Let P be a proposition.

Let Q be a proposition.

Let R be a proposition.

Assume (P → Q); denote this assumption by a.

Assume (Q → R); denote this assumption by b.

Assume P; denote this assumption by h.

Claim: R

Proof.

Using a and the established fact P, conclude Q.

Using b and the established fact Q, conclude R.

∎
