PRShowcase.alternatives

Let P be a proposition.
Let Q be a proposition.
Let R be a proposition.
Assume (P ∨ Q); denote this assumption by h.
Assume (P → R); denote this assumption by hp.
Assume (Q → R); denote this assumption by hq.

Claim: R

Proof.
Use the disjunction (P ∨ Q). Prove the result separately in the P case and the Q case; then conclude R. Each branch keeps its own assumptions.
  This is the assumption h: (P ∨ Q).
  First case: This is the assumption hp: (P → R).
  Second case: This is the assumption hq: (Q → R).

∎
