PRShowcase.injective_comp

Let X be a type.
Let Y be a type.
Let Z be a type.
Let f : (X → Y) be arbitrary.
Let g : (Y → Z) be arbitrary.
Assume f is injective; denote this assumption by hf.
Assume g is injective; denote this assumption by hg.

Claim: (x ↦ g(f(x))) is injective

Proof.
Let x : X be arbitrary.
Let y : X be arbitrary.
Assume (g(f(x)) = g(f(y))); denote this assumption by h.
Injectivity of g, applied to (g(f(x)) = g(f(y))), gives (f(x) = f(y)).
  This is the assumption h: (g(f(x)) = g(f(y))).
Injectivity of f, applied to (f(x) = f(y)), gives (x = y).

∎
