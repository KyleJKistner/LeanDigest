Leanglish · BatchAcceptance.Logic.choose

Let α be a type.
Let P be an element of α → Prop.
Let Q be a proposition.
Assume ∃ x, P x.
Assume ∀ (x : α), P x → Q.
Then Q.

Proof.
Since ∃ x, P x, choose x ∈ α with P x.
  Use the hypothesis h: ∃ x, P x.
Apply the hypothesis use: ∀ (x : α), P x → Q. Substitute x for its object arguments.
  Q
  Use the hypothesis hx: P x.
