Let P be a proposition.
Let Q be a proposition.
Let R be a proposition.
Assume P ∨ Q.
Assume P → R.
Assume Q → R.
Then R.

Proof.
Use the intermediate claim h = h → R. Apply it to the additional premises shown below.
  R
  Reason by cases from P ∨ Q.
    Use the hypothesis h: P ∨ Q.
    Case P.
      Assume h = ⋯.
      Apply the hypothesis hp: P → R.
        R
        Use the hypothesis p: P.
    Case Q.
      Assume h = ⋯.
      Apply the hypothesis hq: Q → R.
        R
        Use the hypothesis q: Q.
  By reflexivity, h = h.
    Use the hypothesis h: P ∨ Q.
